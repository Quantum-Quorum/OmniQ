# Quantum Foundations: Mechanics, Circuits, Chaos & Algorithms

## 1. Mathematical Formalism of Quantum Mechanics

### State Vectors and Density Operators
* **Pure State**: $|\psi\rangle \in \mathcal{H}_d$ with $\langle \psi | \psi \rangle = 1$.
* **Density Matrix**: $\rho = \sum_i p_i |\psi_i\rangle\langle\psi_i|$ with $\mathrm{Tr}(\rho) = 1$ and $\rho \ge 0$.
* **Purity**: $\gamma = \mathrm{Tr}(\rho^2) \in [1/d, 1]$. A state is pure iff $\gamma = 1$.
* **Von Neumann Entropy**:
  $$S(\rho) = -\mathrm{Tr}(\rho \log_2 \rho) = -\sum_i \lambda_i \log_2 \lambda_i$$

### Bipartite Entanglement & Partial Trace
For a bipartite system $\mathcal{H}_{AB} = \mathcal{H}_A \otimes \mathcal{H}_B$:
* **Reduced Density Matrix**: $\rho_A = \mathrm{Tr}_B(\rho_{AB}) = \sum_k (I_A \otimes \langle k|_B) \rho_{AB} (I_A \otimes |k\rangle_B)$.
* **Wootters Concurrence** (for 2 qubits):
  $$\mathcal{C}(\rho) = \max(0, \lambda_1 - \lambda_2 - \lambda_3 - \lambda_4)$$
  where $\lambda_i$ are the square roots of the eigenvalues of $M = \rho (\sigma_y \otimes \sigma_y) \rho^* (\sigma_y \otimes \sigma_y)$ in decreasing order.
* **Peres-Horodecki Negativity**:
  $$\mathcal{N}(\rho) = \frac{\|\rho^{T_B}\|_1 - 1}{2} = \sum_{\lambda_i < 0} |\lambda_i|$$
* **Canonical Bell Basis**:
  $$|\Phi^\pm\rangle = \frac{|00\rangle \pm |11\rangle}{\sqrt{2}}, \quad |\Psi^\pm\rangle = \frac{|01\rangle \pm |10\rangle}{\sqrt{2}}$$

## 2. Open Quantum Systems & Decoherence

Physical qubits are never fully isolated from their environment. Evolution is modeled by the **Lindblad Master Equation**:
$$\frac{d\rho}{dt} = -i[H, \rho] + \sum_k \left( L_k \rho L_k^\dagger - \frac{1}{2}\{L_k^\dagger L_k, \rho\} \right)$$

### Coherence Times ($T_1$ and $T_2$)
1. **$T_1$ (Energy Relaxation / Longitudinal Damping)**:
   * Collapse operator: $L = \sqrt{\Gamma_1} |0\rangle\langle 1| = \sqrt{1/T_1} \sigma_-$.
   * Population decays: $P_1(t) = P_1(0) e^{-t / T_1}$.
2. **$T_2$ (Dephasing / Transverse Coherence)**:
   * Governs off-diagonal coherences: $\rho_{01}(t) = \rho_{01}(0) e^{-t / T_2}$.
   * Fundamental relation:
     $$\frac{1}{T_2} = \frac{1}{2 T_1} + \frac{1}{T_\phi}$$
     where $T_\phi$ is the pure dephasing time. Physical limit: $T_2 \le 2 T_1$.

## 3. Universal Quantum Circuits & Synthesis

### Gate Sets & Universality
* **Clifford Group**: Generated by $\{H, S, \text{CNOT}\}$. Can be efficiently simulated classically in $O(n^2)$ time (Gottesman-Knill Theorem).
* **Universal Fault-Tolerant Gate Set**: Clifford + $T$ gate ($T = \text{diag}(1, e^{i\pi/4})$).
* **Single-Qubit Euler Z-Y-Z Decomposition**:
  $$U = e^{i\alpha} R_z(\beta) R_y(\gamma) R_z(\delta)$$
  where $R_n(\theta) = \exp(-i \theta \sigma_n / 2)$.

### Standard Algorithms
* **Quantum Phase Estimation (QPE)**: Estimates eigenvalue $\theta$ of unitary $U|\psi\rangle = e^{2\pi i \theta}|\psi\rangle$ with precision $O(1/2^t)$.
* **Grover's Search**: Quadratic speedup $O(\sqrt{N})$ using diffusion operator $D = 2|\psi_0\rangle\langle\psi_0| - I$.
* **Variational Quantum Eigensolver (VQE)**: Hybrid algorithm finding ground state $E_0 = \min_\theta \langle\psi(\theta)|H|\psi(\theta)\rangle$.
* **QAOA**: Combinatorial optimization using alternating problem and mixer Hamiltonians $e^{-i\gamma H_C} e^{-i\beta H_B}$.

## 4. Quantum Chaos, Information Scrambling & Thermalization

Quantum chaos characterizes how local quantum information is delocalized across non-local entanglement degrees of freedom.

### Out-of-Time-Ordered Correlators (OTOCs)
Measures the operator growth of initially commuting local observables $W(t) = e^{iHt} W e^{-iHt}$ and $V(0)$:
$$F(t) = \langle W^\dagger(t) V^\dagger(0) W(t) V(0) \rangle$$
$$C(t) = \langle [W(t), V(0)]^\dagger [W(t), V(0)] \rangle = 2 - 2\,\mathrm{Re}(F(t))$$

* **Lyapunov Exponent $\lambda_L$**:
  $$C(t) \sim \frac{1}{N} e^{2 \lambda_L t}$$
* **Maldacena-Shenker-Stanford (MSS) Bound**:
  $$\lambda_L \le \frac{2\pi k_B T}{\hbar}$$
* **Scrambling Time**: Time at which $C(t) \sim O(1)$:
  $$t_* \sim \frac{1}{\lambda_L} \ln N$$

### Random Matrix Theory (RMT) & Level Statistics
Quantum chaotic Hamiltonians exhibit spectral rigidity and eigenvalue repulsion:
* **Level Spacing Ratio**: $r_n = \frac{\min(s_n, s_{n-1})}{\max(s_n, s_{n-1})}$ with $s_n = E_{n+1} - E_n$.
  * Integrable (Poisson): $\langle r \rangle \approx 0.386$ (no level repulsion).
  * Chaotic (Gaussian Orthogonal Ensemble - GOE): $\langle r \rangle \approx 0.536$.
  * Chaotic (Gaussian Unitary Ensemble - GUE): $\langle r \rangle \approx 0.600$.

### Spectral Form Factor (SFF)
$$K(t) = \left\langle \left| \sum_{j} e^{-i E_j t} \right|^2 \right\rangle$$
* Displays the universal chaos signature: **Slope $\to$ Dip $\to$ Ramp $\to$ Plateau**.

### Eigenstate Thermalization Hypothesis (ETH)
Explains how closed, unitary quantum systems thermalize without an external bath:
$$\langle m | O | n \rangle = \bar{O}(E) \delta_{mn} + e^{-S(E)/2} f_O(E, \omega) R_{mn}$$
where $R_{mn}$ is a random variable with zero mean and unit variance.

## 5. Fault-Tolerant Quantum Error Correction (QEC)

* **Stabilizer Formalism**: An $[[n, k, d]]$ code encodes $k$ logical qubits into $n$ physical qubits with distance $d$, defined by an abelian subgroup $\mathcal{S} \subset \mathcal{P}_n$ not containing $-I$.
* **Surface Code**: 2D square lattice of data and ancilla qubits measuring $X$- and $Z$-type star and plaquette stabilizers:
  $$A_s = \prod_{i \in \text{star}(s)} X_i, \quad B_p = \prod_{j \in \text{boundary}(p)} Z_j$$
* **Threshold Theorem**: If physical error rate $p < p_{th} \approx 1\%$, logical error rate decays exponentially with code distance $d$:
  $$P_L \propto \left( \frac{p}{p_{th}} \right)^{(d+1)/2}$$
